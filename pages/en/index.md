# ParrotPoster — auto posting to social networks

**ParrotPoster** is a cloud service for publishing content to social networks: manually from your personal account, automatically from your website or an RSS feed, or via API. Connect your accounts once — after that, posts go to the selected platforms without copying them into each network by hand.

## Four ways to use the service

| Way | Who it is for | Where to start |
| --- | ------------- | -------------- |
| **Personal account** [parrotposter.com](https://parrotposter.com) | Publishing and automation without a CMS | [Getting started](/en/start/) |
| **WordPress plugin** | WordPress sites: the app works right inside the admin panel | [WordPress plugin](/en/start/wordpress.md) |
| **1C-Bitrix module** | Sites on "1C-Bitrix: Site Management" | [1C-Bitrix module](/en/start/bitrix.md) |
| **GraphQL API and API tokens** | Your own scripts, services and integrations | [API](/en/api/) |

All of them use the same account and the same connected social network accounts.

## Features

- **Manual posts** — text and media, several networks at once, scheduled publishing, drafts and a calendar. [Learn more](/en/posts/)
- **Automation** — rules that publish entries from your website or an RSS/Atom feed to social networks on their own: instantly, from the archive and in other modes. [Learn more](/en/automation/)
- **Per-network settings** — every platform has its own publishing options. [Learn more](/en/posts/network-settings.md)
- **Engagement statistics** — engagement figures for published posts. [Learn more](/en/posts/engagement.md)
- **ParrotAI** — a built-in assistant that answers questions about the service and helps with setup. [Learn more](/en/assistant/)
- **GraphQL API and API tokens** — publishing and account management from your own code. [Learn more](/en/api/)
