# ParrotPoster — social network auto-publishing

**ParrotPoster** is a cloud service for posting content to social networks from your site, the web app, or the API. The idea is simple: connect your accounts and a plugin (or the API) once, then your news and posts go to the selected platforms without pasting the same text into every network by hand.

## Features

- **Auto-posting** — publish material from your CMS to connected accounts according to your settings.
- **Multiple platforms** — a single post can be duplicated to several social networks.
- **Web app** at [parrotposter.com](https://parrotposter.com) — sign up, profile, connect accounts, same login as for the plugins; a trial is available to try the service.
- **GraphQL API** — build publishing and account management into your own apps and scripts ([API documentation](/en/api/)).

## Plugins and integrations

| Channel | Description |
| -------- | -------- |
| [WordPress plugin](/en/wordpress/) | Publish from the WordPress admin; install from the plugin directory. |
| 1C-Bitrix | Integration with **1C-Bitrix: Site Management**; the add-on is [in the 1C-Bitrix marketplace](https://marketplace.1c-bitrix.ru/solutions/vettich.sp3/). A full walkthrough is in the [Russian documentation](/ru/bitrix/). |

Both options use the same cloud back end: you sign in to the plugin or add-on with your ParrotPoster account, then configure social accounts and posts from your usual CMS.
