# Connecting Facebook

## Instructions

To connect Facebook, open the page for adding social networks.
On the **Facebook** tab, click **Go to add Facebook**.

![Add account](/images/socials/fb/fb-0.png)

<!-- #region common-auth -->

Facebook will ask you to confirm access for the ParrotPoster service. Click **Continue**.

![Confirmation](/images/socials/fb/fb-1.png)

Choose to grant access to all page lists so you will not have issues when adding new Pages later.
Then click **Continue**.

![Selecting pages](/images/socials/fb/fb-2.png)

Facebook shows which permissions ParrotPoster will have. Click **Save**.

![Permissions](/images/socials/fb/fb-3.png)

Finally, Facebook opens a page where you can manage app access in the future.
You can also open it from your profile settings.
Click **OK**.

![Finish](/images/socials/fb/fb-4.png)

Then select the Facebook Pages you want to connect and click **Add**.

![Selecting pages](/images/socials/fb/fb-5.png)

Done — you have connected a Facebook Page!

<!-- #endregion common-auth -->

## Troubleshooting

<!-- #region common-errors -->

::: details Group list is not shown
Currently ParrotPoster only supports Facebook Pages, not groups.
We may add groups in the future.
:::

::: details Cannot select a profile
ParrotPoster does not publish to personal profiles automatically — only to Pages and (when supported) groups created for that purpose.
:::

::: details Empty list at the page selection step
Check that you are an administrator or moderator of the Page you need.
If the problem persists, email [support@parrotposter.com](mailto:support@parrotposter.com)
:::

<!-- #endregion common-errors -->
