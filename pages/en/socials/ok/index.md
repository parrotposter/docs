# Connecting Odnoklassniki

## Instructions

To connect Odnoklassniki, open the page for adding social networks.
On the **Odnoklassniki** tab, click **Go to add Odnoklassniki**.

![Add account](/images/socials/ok/ok-0.png)

<!-- #region common-auth -->

Odnoklassniki will ask you to confirm access for the ParrotPoster service. Click **Continue**.

![Confirmation](/images/socials/ok/ok-1.png)

Then select the groups you want to connect and click **Add**.

![Selecting groups](/images/socials/ok/ok-2.png)

Done — you have connected an Odnoklassniki community!

<!-- #endregion common-auth -->

## Troubleshooting

<!-- #region common-errors -->

::: details No groups available to connect
Check the following:

- You are a member of the group
- You have moderator or administrator rights
  :::
