# Connecting VK

## Instructions

To connect VK, open the page for adding social networks.
Then click **Connect VK**.

![Add account](/images/socials/vk/vk-0.png)

<!-- #region common-auth -->

VK will ask you to confirm access for the ParrotPoster service. Click **Continue**.

![Confirmation](/images/socials/vk/vk-1.png)

Then select the VK pages you want to connect and click **Add**.

![Selecting pages](/images/socials/vk/vk-2.png)

Done — you have connected a VK page!

<!-- #endregion common-auth -->

## Troubleshooting

<!-- #region common-errors -->

::: details No pages available to connect
Check the following:

- You are a member of the community/page (even if you created it)
- You have moderator or administrator rights
  :::

<!-- #endregion common-errors -->
