# ParrotAI — AI assistant

ParrotAI is the built-in AI assistant of ParrotPoster. It answers questions about how the service works and can help you understand a publishing error. The assistant is available in the cabinet and on the embedded plugin pages when it is enabled for your account.

::: warning Good to know
- An AI replies; a person does not join a ParrotAI conversation.
- Answers may be inaccurate.
- To reach a person, use [Support](./support.md).
:::

## How to open it

Cabinet pages have an **Ask ParrotAI** button (with an **AI** badge). Click it to open the conversation workspace. On a computer it is a panel over the page; on a phone it opens full screen. While ParrotAI prepares a reply, you can keep using the page.

## Conversation

1. Type your question in **Ask a question** and click **Send**.
2. While the reply is being prepared you see the **Thinking…** status.
3. Under every ParrotAI answer there are thumbs-up (**Answer helped**) and thumbs-down (**Answer did not help**) buttons. Click a pressed button again to undo your rating. Rating does not end the conversation — you can continue it at any time. Ratings are used to improve answers. If an answer did not fit, ask a follow-up question.
4. For another topic click **New conversation** (an unsent message is discarded; confirm with **Start new**). Rule: one conversation is for one problem.

## Conversation history

The **History** button opens **Your recent conversations**. Pick a conversation to open it and continue writing; **Load more** loads older ones. A conversation without a title is called **Untitled conversation**. Use **Back to conversation** to return from the list, **Close** to close the panel. If there are no conversations yet — **Start your first conversation**.

## What ParrotAI can do

- explain how to set up and use ParrotPoster features;
- help you understand publishing errors.

## Limitations and messages

If ParrotAI cannot give an answer, it explains why:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| This is outside what ParrotAI can help with | Ask about ParrotPoster |
| ParrotAI is not sure enough to give a reliable answer | Rephrase the question: what you did and what you expected to see |
| This needs a person because it involves access or privacy<br>Billing questions are handled by support<br>This looks like a product issue for support | Contact [Support](./support.md); for billing see also [Plans and billing](/en/billing/index.md) |
| The AI model is unavailable right now | Try again later |
| The AI assistant has reached its usage limit | Try again later |
| ParrotAI could not complete a reply | Ask again |
| Could not send the request. Please try again. | Send again |
| ParrotAI is not available yet | The assistant is not enabled for your account — look for answers in this documentation |

## Hand-off to support

Click **Contact support**: a request form opens with text prefilled from the conversation. More in [Support](./support.md).
