# Support

The **Support** section in the cabinet menu is your conversations with the ParrotPoster team. You can also write to [support@parrotposter.com](mailto:support@parrotposter.com). Questions about payment, refunds and invoices for legal entities are handled by support.

## Create a request

1. Open **Support** and click **New conversation**.
2. Pick a **Category**: **Bug**, **Publishing error**, **How to set up**, **Billing**, **Account**, **Plugin** or **Other**.
3. Describe the issue in **Describe the problem**. Optionally attach a **Related item (optional)**, for example a post.
4. Add **Attachments** with **Choose files**. You can attach up to 5 JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF files, 10 MiB each and 25 MiB total.
5. Click **Send**. Attachments upload first, then the conversation is created.

If you have too many open conversations, you see "You have too many open conversations": mark some as resolved or continue an existing one. For a different issue, create a separate conversation.

::: tip What to include
- what you did and what you expected to see;
- which social network and which account it happened with;
- the full error text;
- a screenshot of the error or the post;
- for payments — the account email and the payment time.
:::

## Draft from ParrotAI

If you click **Contact support** in [ParrotAI](./index.md), the form opens with text prefilled. Edit it before sending. If the draft failed to load you see "Could not load the draft from ParrotAI" — describe the problem manually. A post card with a publishing error has a **Get help with this error** button.

## Statuses and replies

Conversations are split into **Open** and **Resolved**. Statuses:

- **Waiting for support** — waiting for the team's reply;
- **Waiting for your reply** — support replied and is waiting for you;
- **In progress** — the request is being handled;
- **Resolved** — the conversation is closed.

Click **Reply** to answer. A reply in a resolved conversation reopens it. If the issue is solved, click **Mark resolved**. If you do not reply for a long time, a conversation may be completed — it is marked "Completed without a reply".

## Notifications

Support replies arrive by email. You can manage them:

- globally — **Settings → Notifications**, the **Support replies** switch ("Email me when support replies to a conversation"); addresses go in the **Email** field, several separated by commas;
- per conversation — the **Mute email** button (to restore, **Enable email**).
