# Connecting Max

::: tip Beta
The Max messenger integration is in beta. If something goes wrong, email [support@parrotposter.com](mailto:support@parrotposter.com).
:::

There are two ways to connect a Max channel or group in ParrotPoster — with the service bot or with your own bot. Below is the general guide; then what to do in the plugin.

<!-- @include: ../../../socials/max/index.md#max-instructions -->

## Connecting in the plugin

After the preparation steps, in the plugin open **ParrotPoster → Accounts** and click **Connect** for the **Max** messenger.

In the dialog, choose the connection method:

- for the **ParrotPoster bot**, enter the channel/group link or ID and click **Connect**;
- for **your own bot**, paste the token, wait for the channel list, select the channel, and click **Connect**.

Done — the Max account appears in the list, and you can publish from WordPress through ParrotPoster.
