# Connecting Telegram

To connect a Telegram group or channel, you first need to create a bot and then add it as an administrator of the group or channel.

It takes a few more steps than with other networks, but it is still straightforward — take your time.

<!-- @include: ../../../socials/tg/index.md#common-auth -->

## Connecting in the plugin

After the steps above, in the plugin open the social accounts page and click **Connect**.

![Connection page](/images/socials/tg/wp-tg-connect-0.png)

Enter the bot token from BotFather and the link to your group or channel in the corresponding fields.
Then click **Connect**.

![Entering connection details](/images/socials/tg/wp-tg-connect-1.png)

Done — your Telegram account is connected in the plugin.
