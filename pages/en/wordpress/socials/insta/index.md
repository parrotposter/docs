# Connecting Instagram

## Instructions

To connect Instagram, open the page for adding social networks and click **Connect**.

![Add account](/images/socials/insta/wp-0.png)

Enter your Instagram username and password in the corresponding fields and click **Connect**.
The **Proxy** field is optional; nuances are explained below.

::: tip Note
We do not store your password in the ParrotPoster database — we store a session token instead.
The password is only needed for the authorization step.
:::

![Authorization](/images/socials/insta/wp-1.png)

::: warning Attention
Instagram may treat a login as coming from an unusual location.
Confirm the login in the Instagram app or by email.
Then return here and click **Connect** again.
:::
