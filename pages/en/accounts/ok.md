# Connecting Odnoklassniki

The connection uses the official Odnoklassniki authorization. You can publish to **groups** where you are a moderator or administrator.

## Instructions

1. Open the **Accounts** section and click the **Odnoklassniki** button above the account list.

   ![Adding](/images/socials/ok/ok-0.png)

2. If you already have an active Odnoklassniki connection, ParrotPoster offers to **Use** it or create a **New connection**.
3. Odnoklassniki asks you to confirm access for ParrotPoster. Click **Continue**.

   ![Confirmation](/images/socials/ok/ok-1.png)

4. On the "Select communities to add" page, tick the groups you need and click **Add**.

   ![Selecting groups](/images/socials/ok/ok-2.png)

Done: the groups appear in the account list.

## Reconnecting

If an account shows an error, choose **Reconnect** in the account card menu: ParrotPoster tries to restore access automatically and opens the Odnoklassniki authorization again only when needed. See the [accounts overview](./index.md#reconnecting-and-deleting).

## Troubleshooting

::: details No groups available to connect
Check the following:

- You are a member of the group.
- You have moderator or administrator rights.
:::
