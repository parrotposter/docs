# Connecting VKontakte

The connection uses the official VKontakte authorization: you never enter your VK password in ParrotPoster.

## Instructions

1. Open the **Accounts** section and click the **VKontakte** button above the account list.

   ![Adding](/images/socials/vk/vk-0.png)

2. If you already have an active VKontakte connection, ParrotPoster offers a choice ("Existing connection"): **Use** the existing session or **New connection**. Choose "Use" if the pages you need are available in the profile that is already connected.
3. VKontakte asks you to confirm access for ParrotPoster. Click **Continue**.

   ![Confirmation](/images/socials/vk/vk-1.png)

4. On the "Select communities to add" page, tick the VKontakte pages you need and click **Add**. Pages that are already connected are hidden; you can show them with "Show added".

   ![Selecting pages](/images/socials/vk/vk-2.png)

Done: the selected pages appear in the account list.

::: tip Tariff limit
The "Accounts" counter (added / available) is shown on the right. If you select more pages than your tariff allows, the "Add" button becomes inactive and an "Upgrade plan" link appears below.
:::

## Reconnecting

If an account shows an error (for example, access has expired), the account card menu gets a **Reconnect** item. ParrotPoster first tries to restore access through another working VKontakte connection and only asks you to authorize again if that fails. See the [accounts overview](./index.md#reconnecting-and-deleting).

## Troubleshooting

::: details No pages available to connect
Check the following:

- You are a member of the group or page (even if you created it).
- You have moderator or administrator rights.
:::

::: details The wrong VKontakte profile was connected
In the "Existing connection" window choose **New connection** and authorize with the right profile. If VKontakte signs you into another profile right away, sign out of it in the browser and try again.
:::
